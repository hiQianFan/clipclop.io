# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People evaluating a fast clipboard-history utility for macOS or Windows, especially keyboard-oriented developers, designers, and knowledge workers. They need to understand the product quickly and reach the official download without registration or friction.

## Product Purpose

ClipClop's website is the official product introduction and download entry. It succeeds when visitors can experience the essential history-browsing interaction directly in the page, trust that the demonstration reflects the real app, and download the current release through ClipClop's stable Cloudflare/R2 endpoints.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, mechanical, dependable. The site should feel like a focused native utility: dense enough to be useful, calm enough to inspect, and confident without marketing spectacle.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, oversized illustrations, repeated feature-card grids, gamified interactions, and any mock interface that invents capabilities absent from the desktop app.

## Design Principles

- Show the product instead of explaining it with generic feature cards.
- Treat the current desktop app and its design system as the visual source of truth.
- Keep the path from understanding to the official download short.
- Demonstrate only real content types and clearly distinguish static chrome from working interactions.
- Preserve speed, privacy, keyboard access, and calm operational feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic control names, reduced-motion support, and responsive interaction down to a 320px viewport. Light and dark appearance always follow the operating system preference.
